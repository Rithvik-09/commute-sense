# Experiment 2 — Viva Revision

1. **What is a widget?** The basic building block of a Flutter UI.
2. **What is MaterialApp used for?** App-level Material Design configuration and navigation.
3. **What is Scaffold?** A page structure for common Material components.
4. **What is the purpose of AppBar?** It displays a page title and optional actions.
5. **What is the difference between Row and Column?** Row lays out children horizontally; Column lays them out vertically.
6. **What does Stack do?** Places widgets on top of one another.
7. **When is Card useful?** To group related information in a distinct surface.
8. **Why use SingleChildScrollView?** To scroll content that exceeds available space.
9. **What is ElevatedButton?** A Material button with visual elevation.
10. **Why compose small widgets?** It improves reuse, readability, and maintainability.
